    getting around

    move your token

    • F1 opens chat, exits move mode, and focuses the message box.
    • F2 opens chat in move mode for your saved avatar.
    • F3 centers the view on your saved avatar at the current zoom.
    • F4 toggles camera follow mode.
    • With chat focused, F10 opens keyboard settings. The hotbar keys default to the two positions to the right of 0.
    • Use WASD or the alternate movement keys. By default, hold Shift for a half step.
    • In move mode, Z sets a map target. Move the marker with your movement keys and press Space to set it. Use the targeting shortcut again to clear it.
    • Misty Step and Shatter open targeting one tile ahead if they need a target.
    • With quickcast enabled, press a skill hotkey while moving the target marker to cast there and clear the marker.
    • Hold Shift and press the hotkey for Bless, Magic Missile, Burning Hands, Shatter, or Fireball, then choose the spell level on the number row. Esc cancels the level prompt.
    • Q / E turn 15°. Shift+Q / Shift+E turn 90°. R flips. Esc exits move mode.
    • Select one other character token on the map and press a skill hotkey to aim at it.

    move around the map

    • Mouse mode: scroll the wheel over the map to zoom. Hold the wheel down and drag to pan, or drag with the Hand tool.
    • Trackpad mode: scroll with two fingers to pan and pinch to zoom.

    Use the assigned Quickbar shortcuts in move mode to switch between tokens 1–9.

    Assign avatar art, an emote, or a granted spell to slots 1–9 and 0. Use the slot’s assigned shortcut in move mode. Slot 0 defaults to Sleep (AFK).
